Interactive maps of HUD Fair Market Rents — the federal rent benchmarks that set Section 8 housing voucher limits across every county in America.

What are Fair Market Rents?

Every October, the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development publishes Fair Market Rents (FMRs) — estimates of the 40th percentile gross rent for standard-quality units in each market area. These figures determine the maximum rent HUD will subsidize under Section 8 vouchers, and are used widely in housing research and policy.
